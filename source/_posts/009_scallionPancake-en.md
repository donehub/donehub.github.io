I was craving my mom's youmo today, what most people call scallion pancakes. My rental place doesn't have the best kitchen setup, but I figured I'd give it a shot anyway.

-------

<!-- more -->
#### 1. Ingredients

Wheat flour (preferably high-gluten), scallions, sesame oil, peanut oil, salt, and whatever other seasonings you like.

#### 2. Steps

###### 1. Make the dough

Add flour to a bowl (high-gluten flour works best). Pour in warm water gradually while stirring with chopsticks. Once the flour forms flakes, knead it into a dough by hand. Keep the dough on the softer side for good stretchability. Cover with plastic wrap and let it rest for 20 minutes.

![N1pDh9.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1pDh9.jpg)

###### 2. Roll out the dough

After resting, knead the dough into a long strip.

![N19cCj.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N19cCj.jpg)

Scallion pancakes are made by layering the dough, so roll it out as thin as you can.

![N1C3Mq.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1C3Mq.jpg)

##### 3. Add the filling

Drizzle sesame oil over the dough, then fold it over repeatedly until the oil is evenly spread across the surface.

![N1PkY4.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1PkY4.jpg)

Sprinkle chopped scallions and a pinch of salt evenly over the top.

![N1P4NF.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1P4NF.jpg)

##### 4. Shape the pancake

Fold the scallion-covered dough from one corner inward, layering it up.

![N1ZtPK.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1ZtPK.jpg)

Roll the folded dough flat again.

![N1VM7t.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1VM7t.jpg)

##### 5. Pan-fry

Watch the oil temperature. On a home gas stove, low heat and slow is the way to go.

![N1VaBn.jpg](https://s1.ax1x.com/2020/06/20/N1VaBn.jpg)
